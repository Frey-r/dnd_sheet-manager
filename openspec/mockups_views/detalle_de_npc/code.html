Traits & Abilities

Regeneration Passive

Strahd regains 20 hit points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 hit point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If he takes radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of his next turn.

Legendary Resistance 3/Day

If Strahd fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

Shapechanger Action

If Strahd isn't in sunlight or running water, he can use his action to polymorph into a Tiny bat, a Medium wolf, or a Medium cloud of mist, or back into his true form.

Lore & GM Notes

Master of Castle Ravenloft and the darklord of Barovia. He is a tragic and ruthless figure, bound to his land by dark powers. In combat, he relies on hit-and-run tactics, phasing through the walls of his castle to regenerate, separating the party, and exploiting their weaknesses before engaging directly. Never let him fight a fair, static battle.
